03 / 12

A potential target may not exist

An association choice and an existence indicator answer different questions.

\[y=(x,r),\qquad r\in\{0,1\}\]
\(\underline y^j\): a legacy candidate carried from the previous scan.
\(\overline y^m\): a possible new target introduced by measurement \(m\).
\[a^j\in\{0,1,2\},\quad b^m\in\{0,1,2\}\]
\(a^j=m\): target \(j\) produced measurement \(m\).
\(a^j=0\): no detection from that target.
\(b^m=j\): measurement \(m\) came from legacy target \(j\).
\(b^m=0\): new target or clutter.
A new candidate is created for each measurement; its posterior existence may remain very small.

05 / 12

Local factors turn states into association weights

Prediction is a probability vector. The outgoing measurement weights are generally unnormalized.

\[q^j(y,a)=\begin{cases}\mathbf1[a=0]&y=\varnothing\\1-p_D&y\ne\varnothing,\ a=0\\p_D L_a(x)/\lambda_c&y\ne\varnothing,\ a>0\end{cases}\]
\[\beta_j(a)=\sum_y q^j(y,a)\alpha_j(y)\]
\[\beta_1\approx[0.2733,\ 0.9371,\ 0.4758]\]
\[\xi_m(0)=1+B_m,\qquad\xi_m(j)=1\]
\[B_m=\sum_x\frac{\mu_n f_n(x)L_m(x)}{\lambda_c}\]
The 1 is the absent-new-target contribution. The extra mass supports a new detection.

08 / 12

One message leaves out one neighbor

The consistency factor prevents two targets from claiming the same measurement.

\[\Psi^{j,m}(a,b)=\mathbf1\big[(a=m)\Longleftrightarrow(b=j)\big]\]
\[\nu^{[\ell]}_{m,j}(a)\propto\sum_b\Psi^{j,m}(a,b)\,\xi_m(b)\!\prod_{i\ne j}\varphi^{[\ell-1]}_{i,m}(b)\]
\[\varphi^{[\ell]}_{j,m}(b)\propto\sum_a\Psi^{j,m}(a,b)\,\beta_j(a)\!\prod_{n\ne m}\nu^{[\ell]}_{n,j}(a)\]
Excluding the recipient avoids immediately sending its own information back to it.
Each message has two distinct values. The demo rescales nonmatching entries to 1.

Meyer et al. (2018) · p. 232, 243

Today’s new candidates become tomorrow’s legacy set

Messages travel forward through time; there is no backward smoothing pass in this schedule.

Updated legacy candidates
\[\widetilde f(\underline y_k^1),\quad\widetilde f(\underline y_k^2)\]
Updated new candidates
\[\widetilde f(\overline y_k^1),\quad\widetilde f(\overline y_k^2)\]
→
Prediction at the next scan
\[y_{k+1}^j\sim f(y_{k+1}^j\mid y_k^j)\]
After optional pruning, both groups enter the same legacy-target prediction step.
Declaring and pruning targets uses existence thresholds; the demo keeps all candidates so their beliefs stay visible.

11 / 12

Local updates replace a global assignment sum

Each sweep visits target–measurement pairs and reuses compact summaries.

What scales
The two-valued consistency messages admit scalar ratios. With shared sums, one association sweep costs work proportional to the number of candidate pairs.
\[O(n_p n_m)\quad\text{per DA sweep}\]
What the figure does not promise
A fixed number of sweeps does not certify exact posterior marginals.

Continuous-state integration, particle counts, gating, and the number of potential targets also affect total tracking cost.

Meyer et al. (2018) · p. 232–234, 242–244
