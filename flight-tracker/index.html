Flight status and delays

Not retrieved automatically. This page does not have licensed flight-data API access. It does not independently know if this flight is on time, delayed, airborne, landed, cancelled, or diverted.

On the linked tracker pages, compare scheduled times with estimated times (before departure or landing) or actual times (after the event). A positive difference is a delay in minutes. The airline or airport remains the most authoritative source for operational changes and delay reasons.

What do the times mean?

Scheduled
The timetable departure or arrival time.
Estimated
The provider's current prediction, which may change as new information arrives.
Actual
The reported departure or arrival event; landing and arrival at the gate are not necessarily the same time.

Both services provide status and timing information where available. Neither reliably explains the reason for a delay. Automated in-page status and delay figures would require an authorized data API and a server-side integration; this static page links to original records rather than inventing figures.
