How a delay probability would be calculated

Define a delayed departure as actual gate departure − scheduled gate departure > 0. A standard approach is logistic regression:

P(delay) = 1 / (1 + exp(−z))
z = β₀ + β₁r + β₂w + β₃g + β₄v + β₅p + β₆h

Here r is the share of earlier resolved departures delayed at this airport; w is forecast wind speed, g gusts, v visibility, p precipitation, and h this flight’s historical delay rate. Only information available before the prediction time belongs in the model.

The β coefficients must be fitted to matched historical flights and weather, then tested on later dates. There is no universal set of coefficients. This page does not invent weights or turn a few observed flights into a calibrated percentage. Takeoff times cannot substitute for gate departure labels when training this model.
