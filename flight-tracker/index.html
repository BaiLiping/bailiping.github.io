Check historical delay performance and the status of a flight on a specific day, without leaving this page.

Flight number includes airline code (SK1431, BA247). Dates refer to the departure day. Historical performance covers the ten preceding calendar days; for a future departure, it covers the ten days before today.

Delays compare scheduled gate times with actual times. Estimated values are labeled “est.” and excluded from the historical on-time statistics. “No record” does not mean a flight was on time or cancelled. On-time arrival means less than 15 minutes late.

Data requirements: Embedded information requires a secure flight-data API connection. This site does not guess statuses or delays. The implementation supports FlightAware AeroAPI, whose Standard or Premium tier includes historical flight data. Flightradar24's API does not currently provide scheduled times needed to calculate delay figures. The provider may not have every route or flight, and the airline is the authoritative source for operational updates.
