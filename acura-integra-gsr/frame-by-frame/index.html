How the body shape was checked

The build uses Acura’s 2,570 mm wheelbase and 1,710 mm body width. Each exterior camera was fitted to visible features such as the wheel hubs, lights and windshield corners. The workshop photos were held out of the initial body fit. Their camera angles were fitted separately.

For 24 whole-car views, the distance between the photographed and rendered outer boundaries was measured in both directions. Each figure reports the 95th percentile as a percentage of the car’s larger visible dimension. The initial two body fits had median distances of 3.2% and 2.7% with identical cameras. Moving the windshield base forward and raising the rocker trim brought the median for these same views from 2.8% to 2.5%. Each measured frame below includes the current result. The turquoise outlines come from the model in the viewer. Shadows, hidden surfaces and reflections limit the precision of the photo outlines.

Shape shows the current mesh in turquoise. “First body outline” adds the initial silhouette in amber. The close-ups and open-panel views have their own cameras. The other 16 detail and open-panel views were checked visually. The cabin and engine add two comparisons with approximate framing.

Five-speed transmission and connections

The 2000 GS-R parts catalog and Acura’s workshop drawings identify the clutch housing, parallel mainshaft and countershaft, synchronizers, shift forks and differential. The linkage drawing shows the shift rod and extension rod. The axle drawing locates the intermediate shaft and the front driveshaft connections.

The cutaway uses 3.230, 1.900, 1.360, 1.034 and 0.787 for the five forward gear pairs and 4.400 for the final drive. The ratios set relative pitch diameters. Display tooth counts, case dimensions and linkage clearances are approximate.

Factory line drawings supplied the internal gear and linkage references. The search found no factory photographs of those exact assemblies.
