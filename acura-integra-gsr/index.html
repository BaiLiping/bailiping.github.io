Gestures and shortcuts

Click a door, hood, liftback or fuel door to open it. Drag a part to pull its assembly away. Alt-drag moves just the mesh under the pointer. Click a detached part to put it back. On a touchscreen, hold before pulling.

Arrow keys orbit the car or look around the cabin. Plus and minus zoom. E steps through partial and full separation. I toggles the cabin, G opens the fuel door, X toggles X-ray and R resets the car.

Model references
