Model references

Photo-based 1988 AW11 Supercharged reconstruction in Toyota Super Red (3E5) over black leather. The engine lid follows the overhead, rear-quarter and open-lid photographs of the Super Red five-speed. The model has a fixed roof. The photographed car has removable T-bar panels and a TRD exhaust header. The gearbox layout follows Toyota’s E51 repair drawings. Concealed dimensions, cable runs and gear tooth profiles are approximate.
