Geometry and references

This reconstruction uses the U.S. 992.2 Turbo S Cabriolet with paint option 1A, the black fabric roof and the standard 20/21-inch Turbo S wheels. The nine exterior studio frames show Porsche’s configured car from both sides, the front, the rear, three-quarter angles, above and at the front wheel. Five road photographs show the open cabin from the front, rear, both sides and above. The Provence and Ice Grey Metallic press cars have different wheel, finish and interior options. The interior views compare the dashboard and both seating rows.

The axle spacing is 2,450 mm, the front track is 1,583 mm and the rear track is 1,588 mm. Porsche lists a 4,551 mm body length, 1,900 mm body width, 2,033 mm overall width and 1,304 mm height for the launch-generation Cabriolet. The nominal tires are 255/35 ZR20 and 325/30 ZR21. The body, roof, lights, wheels and seats were built for this car. The dashboard, steering controls, mirrors and door trim adapt common 992.2 geometry from this site’s GT3 study. The Turbo S headlamp housings and four-point light guides were modeled for this car.

The nine studio comparisons measure the nearest-edge distance in both directions at 1,920 × 1,080 pixels. An opacity threshold of 248/255 excludes the configurator’s floor shadow. The model threshold of 24/255 includes its translucent glazing. Image-border segments are omitted. The median and 95th percentile describe the registered silhouette. Interior lens positions, seat bolsters, switchgear, the wing supports and the fine optical elements remain approximate. The roof control switches between the raised state and the open endpoint. The lowered-roof state approximates the stowed fabric and covered rear deck.
