Gestures and shortcuts

Tap a door, lid or flap to open it. Drag an assembly to detach it. Alt-drag moves a single part. On touchscreens, hold the part before dragging.

Play the assembly sequence

D opens all four doors. 1–4 operate each door separately. F opens the frunk lid, L the liftgate, and G the charge port.

Model references

The primary studio set shows a Swedish 2023 Long Range AWD in blue with 19-inch Gemini covers. Its plates and towball differ from the US model. The white 2023 and blue 2024 cars provide additional exterior checks.
