Model & what the filter knows

A finite 1D target occupies [c − L/2, c + L/2]. Each return has its own unknown source uᵢ, uniform over [−L/2, L/2], plus independent Gaussian noise: zᵢ = c + uᵢ + vᵢ.

The center prior is Gaussian. The extent prior is lognormal with adjustable median and fixed log standard deviation 0.5. The initial priors are independent; the joint posterior generally couples center and size.

The two displayed observations are deliberately placed 25% and 85% of the way along the selected half of the body. Changing sensor noise changes the likelihood, not those observations. This is a chosen measurement set, not random playback.

The estimator receives the two observations, noise level, and priors. It does not receive the true center, true extent, source positions, or which side produced the detections. Two returns need not identify the full body.

For each possible (c,L), integrate out the two source positions and multiply their likelihoods. Numerical quadrature in (c, log L) gives the joint posterior and its two marginals. The orange curve integrates the other variable's prior and is scaled to fit; it is not an additional observation.

Spatial-source convolution: Granström, Baum & Reuter, Eq. (6), specialized here to a segment. One target, one scan, two associated returns.
