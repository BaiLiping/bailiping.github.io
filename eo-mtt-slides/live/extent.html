Model & source

One moving ellipse, with independent source points uniform over its area and additive Gaussian sensor noise. The tracker starts with the same circular shape prior for every slider setting.

The Gaussian–inverse-Wishart random-matrix filter estimates position, velocity, and the extent matrix from each measurement set. It approximates the uniform source density by a Gaussian with covariance X/4. Shape is inferred from the cloud scatter, with sensor-noise correction.

Single object, known association, fixed count per scan. This simulation does not perform multi-object association. Sliders replay the same random draws under the new settings.

Granström, Baum & Reuter: Eq. (14), Tables IV & IX, describing the Feldmann et al. update and prediction. Symmetric matrix square roots are used here.
