Extent = Gaussian spatial standard deviation. Known extent; 8 returns assigned to one object.

Model & source

zᵢ = x + eᵢ + vᵢ, with independent zero-mean Gaussian extent offsets and sensor errors. The return count is fixed at 8. This is one scalar center update, conditional on extent and association.

The dots are a fixed illustrative cloud centered on z̄. The extent band is also centered there for display; the true center is unknown.

Granström, Baum & Reuter, Eq. (14): the demo uses a 1D Gaussian source model, with its spatial variance represented by σe2.
