Frame-by-frame comparison

Compare the Boost Blue FK8 in 28 views. The exterior views are American Honda’s official 2020 Civic Type R 360° colour-selector renders: 12 frames at 30° steps all round the car and 6 close-ups cut from the same frames. Their cameras were solved from the renders, so the model is drawn from the same position and lens. Honda’s render shows the car about 5 cm lower on its wheels than its real ride height, so the cameras are matched to the body: body lines align, and the model’s wheels sit about 5 cm lower in their arches than the render’s. The cabin and mechanical photographs come from two 2021 Bring a Trailer cars and use approximate framing. Use Wipe to compare the surfaces or Shape to follow the model’s edges across the image.
