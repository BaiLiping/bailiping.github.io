How to read sponsorship and application requirements

Each listing separates visa sponsorship from citizenship, clearance, export authorization, and existing work-permission requirements. These are the posting's stated conditions, not a decision about an individual applicant.

Potential target means no citizenship or clearance restriction was identified in the reviewed text. Not stated means the posting was reviewed but sponsorship was not promised. Not yet verified means the current requirements could not be checked. A sponsorship question on an application form, equal-opportunity wording, or past company sponsorship does not establish sponsorship for this role.

For a U.S. role, a U.S.-person requirement can include permanent residents and certain protected statuses; it is different from U.S. citizenship or security-clearance eligibility. Export authorization may require a separate employer review. A work visa alone does not resolve these access requirements. For roles outside the U.S., confirm the hiring country's work-permit route.

Internship authorization and full-time employment sponsorship may differ. Current openings show teams and skill requirements; a future start date must be agreed with the employer. Senior, staff, and principal titles require a separate experience check.

Official guidance: DOJ export-control hiring FAQ · BIS deemed exports. Always confirm the specific role with the employer.
