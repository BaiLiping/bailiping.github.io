How it works & vehicle specifications

For the GT3, Civic Type R, and Raptor R, enter remaining range in miles and the car’s observed average MPG. Remaining fuel is calculated as range ÷ MPG. MPG starts at 18; the initial 123-mile range is an example, not a live reading.

For the 1988 MR2 Supercharged and 2003 CR-V, enter remaining fuel in U.S. gallons directly. Range and MPG inputs are hidden and are not used for these two vehicles. The gallons field starts blank so you can enter your fuel reading.

GT3 / Civic / Raptor: remaining gallons = remaining miles ÷ MPG
MR2 / CR-V: remaining gallons = the amount you enter
Refill gallons = capacity − remaining gallons
First-pump gallons = (target × capacity − current octane × remaining gallons − second grade × refill gallons) ÷ (first grade − second grade)
Second-pump gallons = refill gallons − first-pump gallons

The volume-weighted octane calculation includes fuel already in the tank. Real octane blending can be nonlinear, and the estimate does not certify fuel quality or suitability. Always follow the vehicle manufacturer’s fuel requirements.

Choose the preset that matches your model year and tank configuration. The default GT3 is the 2025 992.2 with the extended-range tank; other GT3 generations and standard tanks have different capacities.
