An estimate, not a fuel measurement. Range ÷ MPG and fuel-remaining readouts are both approximate, and quantities are rounded to 0.01 U.S. gallon. Stop at the pump’s automatic shutoff; don’t force in the calculated volume.

The math

Octane is treated as a volume-weighted average. Once the tank is full, the fuel already in it and the two pump volumes must add up to the capacity and average out to the target:

\[ O^{\star} C = O_0 V_0 + O_1 V_1 + O_2 V_2, \]
\[ V_0 + V_1 + V_2 = C. \]

Estimating what’s left from the range (the MR2 and CR-V show it directly as fuel remaining), then eliminating \(V_2\), gives the four quantities the calculator reports:

FormulaWith your numbers
  1. \[ V_0 = \frac{d}{m} \]
    \[ V_0 = \text{fuel remaining} \]
    —
  2. \[ V_{\text{fill}} = C - V_0 \]
    —
  3. \[ V_1 = \frac{O^{\star} C - O_0 V_0 - O_2 V_{\text{fill}}}{O_1 - O_2} \]
    —
  4. \[ V_2 = V_{\text{fill}} - V_1 \]
    —

Both pump volumes must lie between zero and \(V_{\text{fill}}\), so the reachable targets are bounded by filling entirely from the lower or the higher grade:

  1. \[ O^{\star} \ge \frac{O_0 V_0 + \min(O_1, O_2)\, V_{\text{fill}}}{C} \]
    —
  2. \[ O^{\star} \le \frac{O_0 V_0 + \max(O_1, O_2)\, V_{\text{fill}}}{C} \]
    —
\(d\)
Range left, miles
\(m\)
Average fuel economy, MPG
\(C\)
Tank capacity, U.S. gallons
\(O^{\star}\)
Target octane for the full tank
\(O_0,\ V_0\)
Octane and volume of the fuel already in the tank
\(O_1,\ V_1\)
Octane and volume from pump 1
\(O_2,\ V_2\)
Octane and volume from pump 2

All octane ratings are AKI. Real blends can be slightly nonlinear, so treat the result as an estimate; it doesn’t certify fuel quality or suitability. Always follow the vehicle manufacturer’s fuel requirements.

Vehicle presets and sources

For the GT3, Civic Type R and Raptor R, enter the range left and your car’s observed average fuel economy. It starts at 18.0 MPG, which isn’t a factory rating, and the 123-mile range is an example, not a live reading.

The 1988 MR2 Supercharged and 2003 CR-V have no range readout, so for them you enter the remaining fuel in U.S. gallons instead. That field starts blank, and range and MPG aren’t used.
