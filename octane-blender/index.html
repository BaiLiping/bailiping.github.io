Calculation method

The calculation uses a volume-weighted octane estimate. The existing fuel contributes to the final target, and the two added grades are solved together to fill the tank.

Target octane × final volume = current octane × current volume + grade A × A gallons + grade B × B gallons

Important: This is an approximate blending calculation. Actual fuel octane blending can be non-linear, pump ratings and fuel formulations vary, and the result is only as accurate as the entered fuel volume and octane ratings. Follow the vehicle manufacturer’s fuel requirements. Do not use this estimate to justify fuel below the required minimum.
