Model references

Photo-matched 2003 USDM Honda CR-V EX 4WD (RD7 pre-facelift) reconstruction in Eternal Blue Pearl (B-96P) with unpainted dark-charcoal lower cladding and a black cloth interior. Equipped with the K24A1 2.4L DOHC i-VTEC inline-four (160 hp, 162 lb-ft), dash-mounted 4-speed automatic with Grade Logic Control, Real Time™ 4WD, 15" 5-split-spoke EX alloys on P205/70R15 tires, power moonroof, right-hinged spare-carrying tailgate, independent flip-up rear glass, and built-in folding cargo picnic table (2,620 mm wheelbase, 4,537 mm overall length, 3,347 lbs curb weight).
