Stockholm

96 photos · 7 clips

A trip through the capital — streets, water, and public art.

This is the horse chestnut, which is poisonous. I thought it is Chinese chestnut, which is eatable.

There are a lot of public statues throughout the city. Apparently, there is such a thing called 1% rule: The 1%-rule was implemented in 1963 by the Stockholm City Council, meaning that one percent of the total cost of built projects (including new construction, conversion, and extension) shall be allocated to publicly accessible artwork.
